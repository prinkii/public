About Prinkii

Reimagining how the
world prints.

We believe printing should be as effortless as sending a message. Prinkii is building the infrastructure to make self-service printing instant, accessible, and delightful — everywhere.

Born from a simple frustration.

Every student, professional, and traveler has felt it — the maddening loop of finding a print shop, waiting in line, wrestling with USB drives, and losing time you'll never get back.

We founded Prinkii to kill that friction entirely. We set out to build a printing experience that feels as seamless as tapping "send" — scan a QR code, upload from your phone, pay digitally, and collect your prints in seconds. No queues. No drivers. No hassle.

Today, Prinkii kiosks are deployed across universities, offices, and co-working spaces — quietly transforming how thousands of people print every day.
